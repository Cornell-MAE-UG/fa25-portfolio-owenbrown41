About

I'm a junior studying mechanical engineering at Cornell, class of 2028. I row on the men's lightweight crew team, where I sit in the stroke seat.

I've spent the last two summers coaching. In 2025 I was head coach at the Anchorage Rowing Association in Alaska, running a program of about 50 rowers. In 2026 I came home to coach the varsity boys at Princeton National Rowing Association, the junior program I grew up rowing for.

On the engineering side, I'm narrowing my interests toward drone design. Outside of class and the boathouse, I sing a cappella with The Men of Last Call and spend as much time outdoors as I can.
