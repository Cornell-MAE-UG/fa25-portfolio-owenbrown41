## The challenge

For this class project, we had to design a mechanism from a rigid bar of fixed length, three pin supports (two of them mounted to the ground), and a linear actuator. The goal was to lift the heaviest possible mass the farthest possible distance off the ground.

## My designs

Those two goals pull against each other, so I made two designs. One lifted the heaviest possible mass. The other lifted a lighter mass a greater distance.
